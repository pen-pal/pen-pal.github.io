The engineer.

I treat infrastructure as a discipline of craft — where architecture decisions matter as much as the code running on top. My work spans the full lifecycle: design, CI/CD automation, security hardening, compliance, and ongoing FinOps — across crypto and blockchain, real-time video and ML inference, and ed-tech.

At ZenLedger I led the SOC 2 and ISO 27001 programme, moved observability off Datadog and replaced the VPN with zero-trust access. At CloudHero I migrated clients to AWS, GCP, Azure and DigitalOcean with near-zero downtime, and at Innovatetech I led the DevOps team behind mySecondTeacher through the COVID-19 surge.

A background in Electrical Engineering combined with ongoing study of Psychology gives me an unusual lens: rigorous systems thinking layered with genuine curiosity about the human dynamics that make engineering cultures thrive.
